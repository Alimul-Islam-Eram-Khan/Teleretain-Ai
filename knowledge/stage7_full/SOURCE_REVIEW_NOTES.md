## What's incomplete on delivery

- Public sitemap and full page-level discovery have **not been crawled in the user's Windows runtime**. Discovery is a local command and requires official robots permission and internet access.
- Every possible subscriber-specific, app-only, promotional, time-limited, postpaid, government, corporate and dynamic custom offer **cannot be guaranteed** from public website content.
- Newly discovered official web pages are placed in **review_queue**, not automatically passed into searchable authoritative content.
- Each approved offer carries `availability="publicly_listed_unconfirmed"`. `sim_type="public"` is *not proof of individual eligibility*.
- The first 14 days of catalog freshness are based on a **dated publicly accessible listing**, not an online purchase confirmation. After that the code refuses stale price assertions.
